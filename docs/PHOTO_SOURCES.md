# Photo sources

Free-licence placeholder photos used on the site. None of them shows CompanyA: they are
illustrative samples of other places (A-157, A-193). Each was checked on its Unsplash page
to be free under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (commercial use
allowed, no attribution required, Unsplash+ photos excluded), to show no identifiable
people, and to name no real resort or hotel (A-194, A-195). Authors are credited here anyway.

Files live in `frontend/public/images/` and are downloaded once with
`frontend/scripts/fetch-photos.sh` (D-57). Room photos are named by room code (A-88). The Plantation Villa's third photo reuses `estate-rows.jpg`.
